About Arsela Gjonaj

Legal Professional & Media Creator

A Bridge Between Worlds

Arsela Gjonaj is an Albanian legal professional and media creator known for her dual career path, balancing a professional background in law with a deep-seated passion for cinema and the arts.

Growing up in Vlorë, Albania, she later moved to Italy to pursue higher education, earning a degree in Law. Arsela describes cinema as an "irreplaceable passion" and uses her platform to act as a "bridge between worlds," connecting her legal training with her creative pursuits and her Albanian roots with her European residence.

Professional Legal Career

Her professional life is characterized by her work in the legal field and her engagement with Albanian public institutions.

  • Specialist for the Municipality of Tirana
  • Lecturer at Albanian University
  • Advisor in the Renewable Energy Sector

Media & Arts

In addition to her legal work, Arsela is a prominent figure in the promotion of Albanian art and culture within the diaspora.

  • ARS ZONE Podcast: Creator and moderator, discussing cinema and Albanian artists abroad.
  • Acting: "Putain d'amendement" and "L'intérêt d'Adam" (2026).
  • Cultural Commentary: Contributor to Shqiptarja.com and TV Klan (Rudina).
